Geospatial Overlap Analysis

Methodology: A 2-mile proximity zone (visualized by the translucent green circles) was calculated extending outward from core elk wintering hubs to chart immediate travel vectors. Findings: The map demonstrates a clear infrastructural overlap. Major highway segments that run directly through these 2-mile elk activity sectors (such as the US-36 mountain gateway and the CO-82 transit corridor) physically dissect the herd's native grazing range. This creates dangerous localized bottlenecks where elk are forced onto active traffic lanes, pinpointing the exact coordinates requiring protective highway fencing and structural overpasses.
